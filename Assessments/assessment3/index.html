Ocean Vuong

Poet, novelist and professor

Ocean Vuong, born in 1988 in Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnam), is a Vietnamese American poet, novelist, and professor. He emigrated to the United States with his family in 1990 and grew up in Connecticut.

His first poems appeared in small literary journals before Night Sky with Exit Wounds (2016) brought him international recognition. His debut novel, On Earth We’re Briefly Gorgeous, followed in 2019, and the poetry collection Time Is a Mother in 2022.
